Clinical governance board

The Lumio Pathways assessment instrument was developed under the oversight of a registered clinical governance board including consultant neurodevelopmental paediatricians and a registered speech and language therapist. Every question, every scoring model and every letter template has been validated by qualified clinicians.

NICE-aligned content

Our questionnaire content is developed in alignment with NICE guidelines NG142 covering autism in children under 5 years and NG207 covering autism in children and young people, alongside RCSLT clinical frameworks for speech and language.

What Lumio Pathways is not

Lumio Pathways is not a diagnostic tool and does not provide clinical diagnoses. It is a clinically informed identification and navigation platform designed to help families access professional assessment sooner and navigate the statutory process more effectively.

Data protection

Lumio Pathways is ICO registered and built in full compliance with UK GDPR and the UK Children’s Code. Your family’s data belongs to you. We will never share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

Lumio Pathways is a parent-administered identification and navigation tool. It is not a substitute for clinical assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. If you have concerns about your child’s development, please also speak to your GP or health visitor.
