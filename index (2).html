The reality for SEND families in England

The system was not built for families like yours.

If you are a parent of a child with special educational needs, you already know how hard this is. The waits are long, the language is complex, the letters go unanswered and the professionals who should be helping you often have no idea what you have already been through. Lumio Pathways exists to change that.

2+ yrs

Average wait for autism assessment

Families wait over two years on average for a community paediatric assessment. Many wait far longer with no support in the interim.

70%

EHCP requests refused first time

Most first-time EHCP requests are refused. Families who know their rights and use the right language are significantly more likely to succeed.

4.8 yrs

Average age at first autism diagnosis

Many children wait until school age or beyond for a diagnosis. Early identification changes outcomes. Every month matters.

1 in 5

Children have special educational needs

Approximately one in five children in England has some form of special educational need. Most families navigate the process entirely alone.

The families waiting longest are often those with the least access to professional advice, the least familiarity with the statutory process and the most to lose from every month of delay. Lumio Pathways was built for those families.

The full picture

The letter is the beginning,
not the end.

Too many platforms stop at the letter. Lumio Pathways does not. Getting a referral letter or an EHCP request is one step in a journey that can last years. We built every part of that journey into the platform, because families need more than a document. They need a companion.

From the moment you complete the assessment to the day your child’s provision is in place and working, Lumio Pathways is with you. We help you understand what to expect, what to say when the system pushes back and how to make sure your child gets what they are entitled to.

Platform features

Everything in one place. Nothing left out.

Stage 1 launches with the core identification and navigation tools. Stage 2 brings EHCP gap analysis and full coordination. Stage 3 opens to schools, clinicians and NHS commissioners.

Stage 1

Clinically validated assessment

12 questions across five developmental domains. Developed under the oversight of a clinical governance board including consultant neurodevelopmental paediatricians and a registered speech and language therapist. Age-appropriate from 18 months to 12 years.

Stage 1

Full strengths and needs profile

Your child’s strengths and needs across communication, social interaction, sensory processing, learning and emotional regulation. Colour-coded domain scores. Downloadable PDF. Ready to share with any professional.

Stage 1

GP referral letter

Clinically informed GP referral letter auto-generated from your child’s profile. Professional language that GPs take seriously. Based on assessed need, not on parental description alone.

Stage 1

EHCP assessment request letter

Statutory EHCP assessment request under Section 36 of the Children and Families Act 2014. Accurate statutory references. Clear, professional and ready to submit the same day you complete the assessment.

Stage 2

EHCP provision gap analysis

Intelligent analysis of your child’s EHCP identifies gaps between identified needs and stated provision across Sections B, E and F. Know exactly what to challenge before the annual review.

Stage 2

Home, school and therapy coordination

Shared weekly clinical targets across home, school and speech and language therapy. One platform, one picture, everyone aligned around your child. Progress tracked across all settings.

Stage 2

Community and peer support

A moderated community of SEND families navigating the same journey. Share experiences, ask questions and find support from families who understand. Professionally moderated to maintain safety and quality.

Clinical credibility

Built with clinicians. Validated with care. Used with confidence.

Clinical governance board

The Lumio Pathways assessment instrument was developed under the oversight of a registered clinical governance board including consultant neurodevelopmental paediatricians and a registered speech and language therapist. Every question, every scoring model and every letter template has been validated by qualified clinicians.

NICE-aligned content

Our questionnaire content is developed in alignment with NICE guidelines NG142 covering autism in children under 5 years and NG207 covering autism in children and young people, alongside RCSLT clinical frameworks for speech and language.

What Lumio Pathways is not

Lumio Pathways is not a diagnostic tool and does not provide clinical diagnoses. It is a clinically informed identification and navigation platform designed to help families access professional assessment sooner and navigate the statutory process more effectively.

Data protection

Lumio Pathways is ICO registered and built in full compliance with UK GDPR and the UK Children’s Code. Your family’s data belongs to you. We will never share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

Lumio Pathways is a parent-administered identification and navigation tool. It is not a substitute for clinical assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. If you have concerns about your child’s development, please also speak to your GP or health visitor.
